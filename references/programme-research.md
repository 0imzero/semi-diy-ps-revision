# Programme research workflow

Use this route separately for every target degree. Programme facts are time-sensitive: start from the official URL supplied by the applicant and browse current official sources. The output is a programme decision memo, not copied brochure text.

## Required inputs

Read:

- the parent `Applicant_Master_Profile.md`;
- the programme folder's `Programme_Info.md`;
- the applicant-supplied official programme URL;
- the official prompt and word limit when available.

If the official URL is missing, stop this route and ask for it. Do not substitute an aggregator or an inferred programme page. If the portal prompt or word limit is unavailable publicly, continue the research and mark that field for portal confirmation.

## Official-source sequence

Follow relevant links from the supplied official page in this order:

1. programme homepage and director's description;
2. curriculum, handbook or university catalogue;
3. official admissions, FAQ, schedule and application instructions;
4. capstone, project, internship, exchange and career pages;
5. official faculty, research-centre or student-work pages when material.

Use non-official material only as a lead, never as proof of curriculum, eligibility, availability or outcomes. Save direct links and the access date. When sources conflict, prefer the more specific and current official source and record the conflict.

## Identify what the degree actually trains

Before mapping the applicant, determine:

- the professional or intellectual problem the programme trains students to solve;
- whether it is taught, research or hybrid;
- the intended student and expected preparation;
- its centre of gravity and difference from adjacent degrees;
- its balance of theory, analytics, practice, implementation and professional judgement;
- the realistic capabilities and entry directions it supports.

Do not infer the programme thesis from its title alone.

## Reconstruct resources accurately

Distinguish required course, elective, optional track, capstone, consulting project, thesis, workshop, company visit, internship support, guaranteed internship, exchange and double degree. Record credit requirements and availability conditions when relevant.

Never turn “at least” into “exactly”; do not call an elective compulsory, a capstone a thesis, or an opportunity guaranteed without official support. Recheck terms such as “new”, “mandatory”, “partner” and “placement”.

## Map applicant fit

Create an evidence map:

| Programme signal | Applicant evidence | What it proves | Remaining gap | PS use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Choose evidence by relevance, not prestige. Separate:

- what the applicant actually did;
- what the experience reasonably taught them;
- a possible explanation not yet established;
- the future skill the degree should supply.

Recommend one central PS thesis and two or three experiences that form a progression. Name programme resources only when they answer a demonstrated gap. Also state which impressive but irrelevant evidence should be left out.

## Output

Write `<Programme_Folder>/Programme_Research_Memo.md`:

```markdown
# Programme Research Memo — University / Programme

> Intake: ...
> Updated: YYYY-MM-DD

## Official Sources
## Programme Thesis
## Intended Student and Admissions Signals
## Format and Curriculum Structure
## Relevant Courses and Resources
## Experiential Learning
## Difference from Adjacent Programmes
## Applicant Fit Map
## Recommended PS Thesis
## Recommended Evidence
## Evidence Not to Force into This PS
## Prompt and Word Limit
## Questions Requiring Portal Confirmation
## Fact Risks and Update Notes
```

Every unstable claim that may appear in the PS should be traceable to a direct official source. Paraphrase source content; quote only when exact wording matters.

## Completion condition

This route is complete when the memo explains the programme's selection logic, connects it to the applicant's evidence and gaps, records direct official sources, and makes a concrete PS positioning recommendation. Do not modify the PS unless the user also requests a review route.
