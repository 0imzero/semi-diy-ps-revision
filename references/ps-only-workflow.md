# PS-only workflow

Use this route when the applicant has an existing PS but no CV, master profile or preparation pack is ready. Missing materials reduce the range of facts that can be cross-checked; they do not prevent a useful admissions review.

## Build the working evidence base

Use the available sources in this order:

1. explicit user statements and confirmations;
2. the accepted body of the current PS as the working account of the applicant's experience;
3. Word comments, tracked changes and user notes as revision intentions, not accepted prose;
4. current official programme information;
5. older drafts and review notes when they help explain a decision.

Do not force the applicant to build `Applicant_Master_Profile.md` before beginning. Ask only questions whose answers would materially change the thesis, evidence choice, credibility or programme fit. Cross-document consistency can be checked later if a CV or other materials become available.

## Review sequence

1. Research the programme from the official URL supplied by the user. Identify the programme's actual training, distinctive resources and credible applicant fit.
2. Extract the current PS, comments and tracked changes when the source is DOCX. Keep accepted body text separate from comments and rejected edits.
3. Reconstruct the admissions case from the PS itself: memorable applicant identity, central thesis, evidence chain, ability gap, programme fit and career direction.
4. Judge every existing comment from scratch. Keep only feedback that would materially strengthen content, structure, credibility or fit.
5. Add the few missing high-impact comments. Do not comment for coverage, stylistic preference or reassurance.
6. If the user wants to proceed one by one, discuss one comment or paragraph at a time and wait for their decision before moving to the next.
7. After a revised version arrives, reconstruct it independently and classify earlier issues as solved, partly solved, unresolved or newly introduced.
8. When useful, provide a faithful Chinese back-translation of the revised English so the applicant can inspect meaning and emphasis. Treat this as semantic review, not a new literary rewrite.
9. Leave exhaustive file hygiene, exact limits and cross-document consistency to final review.

## Permitted revision latitude

The goal is a persuasive and recognisably human application essay, not a defensive record of every qualification.

Within the facts supplied by the applicant, you may:

- restructure, merge, cut, expand or reweight experiences;
- rewrite an experience around a sharper decision, observation, tension or learning point;
- make an implied mechanism, contribution, reflection or ability gap explicit;
- replace report-like technical detail with the meaning it supports;
- enrich a thin description with plausible candidate detail for the applicant to confirm;
- preserve the document adviser's voice and creative choices when they already work.

When the user explicitly authorises flexibility for an FYP, project or scene, you may develop candidate mechanisms, examples, sequence, framing and reflection beyond the wording already present. Present these as proposed narrative content, not independently verified history. The applicant's confirmation is sufficient to adopt them; do not add visible disclaimers to the PS solely because the detail was developed during revision.

Do not invent fixed biographical or institutional facts: employer, formal role, award, metric, completed outcome, degree requirement, named course or guaranteed career result. If changing one of these facts would strengthen the essay, ask the applicant instead.

## Non-defensive review standard

- Do not weaken a clear, user-confirmed statement merely because a hypothetical reader could challenge it.
- Do not add team-scope, causality or certainty caveats unless the distinction materially affects credibility or meaning.
- Do not explain in every comment that external verification is unavailable.
- Do not turn a vivid experience into abstract risk-managed language.
- Do not confuse truthfulness with exhaustive qualification. A PS may select, compress and frame real experience for persuasive effect.

Use fact checking as a guardrail. The main review question remains: does this version create a coherent, memorable and programme-relevant admissions case?

## Human-voice standard

Retain unevenness, specificity and personal emphasis when they make the writer sound real. Avoid converting every paragraph into the same polished cadence, adding generic transitions or replacing concrete language with fashionable abstractions. Follow the existing writer or adviser unless a material change requires new prose.
